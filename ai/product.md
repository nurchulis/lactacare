# Product Overview: Lacta Care

## Purpose
Lacta Care is a mobile-first Progressive Web App designed specifically to track baby feeding and pumping activities for new mothers.

## Target User
Exhausted, sleep-deprived new mothers who need to quickly log and monitor their feeding/pumping schedule without mental overhead.

## Core Problems Solved
- Remembering when the last feeding or pumping occurred.
- Keeping track of breast milk (ASI) and formula expiration times.
- Eliminating the friction of traditional tracking apps that require logins, complex forms, or multiple taps to log a simple event.

## Key Features & UX Philosophy
- **Fast:** Immediate load times and instant logging.
- **One-handed usage:** Large, accessible touch targets designed for use with a single thumb while holding a baby in the other arm.
- **Minimal Friction:** Max 1-2 taps to log any activity. Zero typing required.
- **Visual Status:** Clear visual indicators (Green, Yellow, Red) and countdown timers to show time remaining before expiration or next scheduled pump.
- **Emotionally Supportive UI:** Soft pastel colors and a friendly, non-clinical tone to reduce stress.
